# Annotated Bibliography: Simulation Frameworks & Benchmarks for Robot Learning

---

### Mittal, M. et al. (2023). “Orbit: A Unified Simulation Framework for Interactive Robot Learning Environments.” *IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters* 8 (6): 3740‑3747.  
Orbit introduces an extensible simulation stack—built on NVIDIA Omniverse—that unifies robot dynamics, sensory realism, and task definition under a single Python API. The framework lowers the barrier to reproducible robot‑learning research by bundling standardized assets, curriculum‑learning utilities, and evaluation dashboards, enabling rapid prototyping from manipulation to legged locomotion.

---

### NVIDIA Corporation (2025). *NVIDIA Isaac Sim Documentation.*  
Isaac Sim is NVIDIA’s next‑generation, GPU‑accelerated robotics simulator atop Omniverse, offering photorealistic rendering, physics based on PhysX, and turnkey sensor models (LiDAR, RGB‑D, event cameras). The online documentation details APIs, environment‑building workflows, and RL‑specific features—such as domain randomization and synthetic‑data pipelines—that bridge simulation and real‑world deployment.

---

### Lu, H. et al. (2024). “GarmentLab: A Unified Simulation and Benchmark for Garment Manipulation.” arXiv:2411.01200.  
GarmentLab presents a cloth‑simulation benchmark with parameterized garments, perception tasks, and manipulation goals that span flattening, folding, and dressing. By standardizing tasks and metrics, the authors create a common testbed that exposes current RL and imitation‑learning algorithms’ shortcomings on highly deformable‑object manipulation, driving progress toward more reliable household‑robot skills.
